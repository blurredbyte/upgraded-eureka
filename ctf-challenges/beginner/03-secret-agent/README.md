# Secret Agent

**Category:** OSINT
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Points:** 100

## Description

One of our secret agents just sent us a picture from their latest mission. We need to identify the city where the agent is currently located. The flag is the name of the city in the format `ICIMS{CityName}`. For example, if the city is London, the flag would be `ICIMS{London}`.

The image file (`see_image_placeholder.txt`) contains a picture of a very famous, large, red-orange suspension bridge. The bridge is partially enveloped in fog. In the background, you can see a body of water and some hills.

Find the city and submit the flag!

## Hints

*   The bridge is a very famous landmark in the United States.
*   The bridge is known for its distinctive color and art deco styling.
*   What major city is famous for its fog and this bridge?
