# The CEO's Favorite

**Category:** OSINT
**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Points:** 200

## Description

Our company's CEO, John Smith, is a bit of a celebrity. He's very active on social media. We need you to do some research on him for a security assessment.

Your task is to find the name of his first pet. This is often used as a security question, so it's a good piece of information to know.

We've found his main social media profile, which is simulated in the `social_media_profile.txt` file in the `files` directory.

The flag is the pet's name, in the format `ICIMS{PetName}`.

## Hints

*   Read the social media profile carefully. Look for posts about his past, his childhood, or his family.
*   People often post pictures of their pets. Look for old pictures.
*   The post you are looking for might be a "throwback" post.
*   Don't just look at the most recent posts. You might need to dig deep into the history.
